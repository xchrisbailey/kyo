Kyō means today, and today is all Kyo shows: your tasks, your habits2/3, your memos.

Why only today

I wanted a planner that only shows today. Not the week, not the month, just the day I’m in.

So that’s all Kyo opens on. What I don’t finish carries forward to tomorrow, and anything that can wait sits in Later until its day.

There’s no account and no server. Your tasks, habits and memos stay on your devices, and your iPhone and Apple Watch sync directly with each other.

I built it for my own days first. It’s free, it’s open source, and it’s still in beta.

Questions

  • Yes. Kyo is free and open source, and the code is on GitHub.

  • It’s in a TestFlight beta that isn’t public yet. Watch the repo for a public build, or build it from source with Xcode.

  • iOS 27, iPadOS 27 or watchOS 27.

  • Your iPhone and Apple Watch sync directly with each other. iCloud sync isn’t built yet, so an iPhone and an iPad each keep their own data.

  • It reads your events to show Today’s Schedule. It never changes them and never stores them, and you choose which calendars appear.

  • Only on your device. Apple Intelligence writes memo titles and suggests tasks from memos. Everything works without it.
