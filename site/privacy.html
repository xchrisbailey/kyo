Kyo GitHub

Privacy

Kyo has no account, no server, and no analytics, and it doesn’t track you.

Your data

Your tasks, habits and memos are stored on your devices. Your iPhone and Apple Watch sync directly with each other. Nothing is sent to a Kyo server, because there isn’t one.

On-device features

Speech and AI features run on your device.

What Kyo asks for

  • Microphone to record voice memos.
  • Camera to take memo photos.
  • Calendars to show the Schedule on Today. Kyo only reads them and never changes them.

Kyo asks for each one only when you first use that feature.
